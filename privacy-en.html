Privacy Policy

Attention Training (AttentionGame-Mini)

Effective date: 2026-11-05

中文

1. What this policy says

This app is an attention training tool that runs on your own phone. This policy explains what it collects, what it does not, and what control you have. The short version: we do not collect your personal information.

2. What we do not collect


· No account to sign up for, and we collect no phone number, name, email address or identity document.
· We do not read device identifiers (IMEI, ANDROID_ID, OAID and similar).
· We do not read your location, and we do not use your contacts, camera or microphone.
· We use no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.
· We do not sell or share your information with anyone — we simply do not have it.

3. The permissions this app asks for

This app asks for one system permission only: network access (INTERNET). That is all it is for: when you turn on the comparison yourself and tap Upload, it sends one training result. Beyond that, the app never makes a network request. In flight mode everything works just the same.

4. Where your training data is stored

Your training records, your settings and your practice log are all stored on this device. We have no copy of them. That also means: uninstalling the app deletes this data, and we cannot recover it for you. If you want to keep it, make your own backup before uninstalling.

5. The only feature that goes online: the comparison

The app only goes online when you turn on the comparison yourself and tap Upload. What gets uploaded is limited to: the training type, your result (stages completed, correct answers, average reaction time), the province you picked, and the upload time. What does not get uploaded: the per-question details of your session, your device information, and anything that identifies you.

6. About the anonymous device code

So that the comparison can tell that one person counts once, instead of counting the same person several times, the app creates a random number on your own device. That number comes from the system secure random source. It is not taken from any device information the system holds, and it cannot be traced back to your device. It is used for two things only: keeping one person counted once, and slowing down repeated uploads. Clear the app data or uninstall the app, and a new number is generated. Your earlier uploads can then no longer be linked to that number.

7. What you control


· You can clear all local data at any time under Settings → My Data.
· You can also choose never to turn on the comparison. Then the app makes no network request, and your use of it stays entirely on this phone.
· Neither of these needs you to contact us.

8. About children

This app is designed for adults, and it collects no information from children under 13. If you find that a child used this app without a parent or guardian agreeing, please contact us.

9. Policy updates

If this policy changes, the change will come with an app update. If a change means we start collecting new information, that will be shown clearly in the app.
